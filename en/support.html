Support

Tempo shows your average speed from the moment you tap Start, together with the elapsed time and the distance. It is made for stretches of road with average speed checks.

Safety first

This app is for information only. Follow traffic laws and never exceed the speed limit. The values shown are GPS-based estimates; they can differ from official measurements and are not legal evidence. Do not handle your phone while driving: start the measurement while stopped, or let a passenger do it.

How to use

The app shows the same steps on the “How to use” page of its info screen.

Before driving

  • On first launch, read the safety notice. When asked for location access, tap “Allow While Using App”; keep Precise Location on.
  • Choose the speed limit and the zone length on the main screen. Both are optional. With a limit, the app warns when the average goes above it; with a length, the measurement stops by itself at the end of the zone; with both, it also shows a recommended speed.
  • During a measurement, only a passenger should change the limit or the length, never the driver.
  • Before the zone, wait until the top of the screen shows “GPS good”.

During the measurement

  • Where the zone begins, let a passenger tap Start, or start the measurement yourself while stopped. Siri can start it too (see below).
  • For about the first 10 seconds, a thin ring fills in place of the average and “Measuring…” is shown. Then the average appears; it is 0 while the car has not moved yet.
  • “--” means that no reliable value can be shown at the moment. Without GPS, the values turn grey and stop updating until the signal is back.
  • After Start, you can lock the screen or open another app: the measurement goes on, and its values also show on the Lock Screen.

At the end of the zone

  • To stop, hold the button for about a second until it fills up. A short vibration confirms it, and the result stays on screen.
  • With a zone length, the measurement stops by itself 200 m after the end of the zone and shows “End of zone reached”. This can be turned off in Settings.
  • “New measurement” clears the result and starts the next measurement right away. The limit stays and the length starts empty; while stopped, both can be prepared in the “Next measurement” row.

With Siri

You can also use Tempo by voice. Say to Siri:

  • “Start Tempo”
    Opens Tempo and starts a measurement.
  • “Stop Tempo”
    Stops the measurement.
  • “What is my average speed in Tempo”
    Tells you the average.

Whether Siri speaks its answers depends on “Siri Responses” in the Siri section of the iPhone's Settings. To hear them, choose spoken responses there.

The first time, iOS may ask once to turn on Tempo's shortcuts for Siri, and Siri may ask once which app you mean.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the first value take a few seconds?

The first value appears about 10 seconds after Start; until then a thin ring fills in its place. This keeps the unsteady GPS values right after Start off the screen. While the car is standing still, the average shows 0.

Why is the average rounded up?

So that the screen never shows less than the measured average: 100.2 km/h is shown as 101.

What does “Measurement may be unreliable” mean?

The GPS signal was interrupted during the measurement. The road driven during the gap is unknown, so the real average speed may differ and may even be higher than shown. The note stays on screen until the next measurement.

Does it keep measuring with the screen locked?

Yes. After you tap Start, the measurement continues with the screen locked or another app open; iOS then shows the location indicator. GPS uses extra battery, so on long drives it helps to keep the iPhone charging.

Why can the value differ from an official measurement?

Tempo estimates your position with GPS. Small GPS errors, signal interruptions and the exact points where you start and stop the measurement all affect the result. The values shown are estimates and are not legal evidence.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Tempo works without an internet connection and sends no data.

The app says “Location access is off” or “Precise Location is off”.

Tap “Open Settings” in the app, or open Settings > Apps > Tempo > Location; choose “While Using the App” and turn on Precise Location. Location Services must be on as well (Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services).
