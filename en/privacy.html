Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Tempo shows your average speed over a stretch of road, for example in an average speed check zone. This policy explains which data the app uses and what happens to it. In short: your location never leaves your iPhone.

Who is responsible

Tempo is developed by Ali Doğan Aydın, an individual developer.

Contact: adatech.tr@gmail.com

Location

  • Your location is used only to calculate average speed, current speed, distance and time. The recommended speed and the automatic stop at the end of the zone are calculated from these values.
  • The app asks only for “While Using the App” location access, never for “Always”. Measuring also needs Precise Location to be on.
  • While the app is open, location is used so that GPS is ready when you tap Start. With the screen locked or another app open, location is used only while a measurement is running; iOS then shows the location indicator.
  • Locations are kept only in the iPhone's memory during the current measurement. They are never saved, and no trip or route history is kept. They are gone when you start a new measurement or the app is closed.
  • Your location is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Data stored on your iPhone

The app stores only its settings on your iPhone (safety margin, warning tone, spoken updates and their interval, automatic stop) and two notes: whether you have read the safety notice and whether a measurement was interrupted. The speed limit and the zone length are not stored. None of this contains location data, and all of it is removed when you delete the app.

Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

While a measurement is running, the average speed, time, distance, speed limit and recommended speed also show on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island (a Live Activity). Anyone who sees your iPhone can read them there without unlocking it. No location is shown. You can turn this off in Settings > Apps > Tempo > Live Activities.

Sounds

Tempo can play a short warning tone and speak short updates through your iPhone. Both can be turned off in the app's settings, and spoken updates stay off unless you turn them on. Spoken updates use the voices built into iOS. The app never uses the microphone.

Siri

You can start and stop a measurement and ask for the average speed with Siri. Siri is a service of Apple: what you say to Siri is processed by Apple; see Siri, Dictation & Privacy. Tempo only carries out the command and gives Siri a short answer, for example the average speed. It sends nothing to the developer.

No data collection

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No ads, no tracking, no usage analytics and no third-party software kits (SDKs).
  • The app works without an internet connection and sends no data. The developer therefore receives no data about you or your trips from the app.
  • If “Share With App Developers” is on in your iOS settings, Apple may share crash data and statistics about how the app is used with the developer. This is done by Apple, not by the app; you can turn it off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Your choices and rights

  • You can change or turn off location access at any time in Settings > Apps > Tempo > Location.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.
  • The app sends no personal data about you to the developer, so the developer has no app data to show, correct or delete.
  • If you have questions, write to adatech.tr@gmail.com.

Email

If you email the developer, your email address and your message are used only to answer you, and they are deleted if you ask. Emails are stored in Google's Gmail service; see the Google Privacy Policy.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The website itself uses no cookies and no analytics.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, this policy is updated before the new version is released, and the date at the top changes.
